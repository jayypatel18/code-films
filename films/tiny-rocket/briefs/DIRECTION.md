# Direction notes, TINY ROCKET

Every line of this film's picture and sound code was written by Fable 5.1 (one agent for the picture, one for the sound), from the prompt in `../PROMPT.md` and the briefs in this folder. An Opus 5.5 session wrote that prompt and the briefs, and between passes it sent notes in words: what read wrong on screen, never code. These are those notes, in order, shortened.

**Look test.** Keep the painted dusk sky, the hill, the grass and the tree, and the panda's face. Fix: the rim light was an offset halo outside the ink line, like a sticker; the body was a flat slab with tube arms and rectangle legs; make the knot and paws the subject of shot 1; give the rocket its handmade build and a taller silhouette; paint clouds as masses; real roofs for the village.

**After the first full film.** Not final yet, by the prompt's own rule. The climax read as flat vector mountains, white stick rivers and a blob star: paint it at the level of the hill, with ranges in haze, a sea of clouds and a brilliant star with a long trail. The close-up body: fur in cel shapes, paws with toes. The title: brush lettering with a loaded start and dry breaks, even spacing. The rocket a real rocket silhouette. The village as clustered roofs. Vary the cockpit close-ups, smoke as wisps, visible soot.

**Mux.** The Opus session joined Fable's soundtrack to Fable's picture with ffmpeg (stream copy), for the full film and the X cut.
