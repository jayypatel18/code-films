# Direction notes, THE LAST PAGE

The film was made by four Opus 5.5 agents (A1 lead and fox, A2 backgrounds, A3 sound, A4 paper), each from a brief in this folder, plus Chetas's prompt (`../PROMPT.md`) and the house style in `COMMON.md`. A fifth Opus 5.5 session directed: it wrote the briefs and `timeline.py`, reviewed stills at every gate and sent notes in words. It wrote no drawing or sound code. These are those notes, in order, shortened.

**Timeline (before any agent started).** The shot list from the prompt split into 16 cuts with plate sizes, cameras, anchors for the floor, lectern, ledge and page, light per cut, and cue times for picture and sound.

**A1, fox model sheet.** Model sheet approved. The cut 3 composite: head too big and ears cut off; the lantern light was a pasted ellipse; the face one flat area; the ruff a lavender mass. Make the fox smaller with headroom, light planes facing the lantern with a hard warm tone plus a soft grade, add designed shadow shapes and fur strands. Slightly bigger paws and more fur at the legs.

**A2, first two plates.** Painter approved (tower, pines, books). The hall floor read as a 3D tile grid, the moon pool was clipped white, the composition frontal and symmetric with a black top half: paint worn flagstones, a soft moon pool with the stone showing through, a vast hall with broken symmetry. The cloud sea read as a repeating pattern: design big billows shrinking to the horizon with moonlit rims.

**A4, paper birds and the great bird.** Small folded page birds approved. The great bird read as tiles on a tube: feathers made of pages (fanned primaries, overlapping rows, a size gradient), a graceful neck and a clear head, haze and shedding pages for scale, keep it in side or three quarter view through the arc, loose pages that curl instead of confetti, a visible blast on the wingbeat. Then: the vortex read as an even texture: make two or three clear spiral bands with dark gaps, bigger birds near the lens.

**Rough cut 1, to A1.** Defocus and darken the library behind the close-up; draw the cut 4b and 9b paws as real legs, the page as paper with a curled corner; slow the walk to a padding walk; the first frame of the X cut must show the fox moving.

**Rough cut 1, to A2.** A dark defocused library for cut 3; a real giant book on a carved lectern; light crashing through the dome instead of a starburst; a stone balcony instead of grey blocks; painted baluster shadows instead of regular stripes.

**Pass 2 and 3, to A1.** The ink bird paw read as a glove, then a fist: crop to the toes with the ink flowing from under them. The ruff: cream with designed cool shadow clumps, no pink blob. The sunrise fox bigger and backlit with fur streaming. The paw well onto the title page.

**Pass 4, to A1.** The half closed drawing in the blink read sullen: lids come down soft and even, or drop the closing half drawing. Then picture lock.

**A2, last notes.** Sunrise clouds read as bubble wrap: cumulus banks of different sizes with gold linings and long violet shadows. The sunrise parapet and tower as real stone. The dome as old copper with the moment led by the light. Round bokeh discs behind the close-up.

**A3, sound.** Re-time every effect to the animation's event file; lift the quiet parts about 4 dB for phones; keep the E major ending; an audible held breath in the stillness; less synthetic strings, horns and choir; a cold open sound for the X cut.

**X cut edit.** The director session cut the X version from the finished master with ffmpeg: the cold open from cut 3, then an 8 frame dissolve into the opening shot at full exposure (the film's own one second of black and fade up is skipped in the X version only).
